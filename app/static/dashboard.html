Test call — audition voices

Property fields power the personalized opening: "మీరు Sri Sai PG owner గారేనా?" → "మీ PG ని Google Maps లో చూశాను…". Leave empty to hear the generic opening. Female voices: kavya, shreya, pooja, ritu · Male: shubh, gokul, aditya, rohan.

Background sound plays under Kavya for the whole call so the line doesn't sound dead. Preview plays the bed at the exact level callers hear, with a line of her voice over it. The number is the volume: 0.06–0.12 sounds natural, 0.2 is clearly present, and louder is refused — a bed near speech level garbles Kavya's words and echoes off the owner's handset into our speech recognition. Your pick applies to this test call only — set AMBIENT_ENABLED / AMBIENT_SOUND / AMBIENT_VOLUME in .env to make it the default for every call.
